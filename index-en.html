Before you startTeams Desktop and compatible drivers; recommended: 32 GB RAM, internal SSD and 25 GB free for the parts, ZIP and extraction. EXE is unsigned; a second clean-PC check is pending.

Place all four ZIP parts and JoinPortable.cmd in the same folder. The script checks sizes and SHA-256. Then extract the archive to an internal SSD.

Legal notes: only with the consent of everyone involved

The app reads Teams live captions and, on request, analyzes a part of the shared screen. Obtain the consent of everyone involved beforehand and follow your organization’s rules. Secretly recording the non-publicly spoken word can be a criminal offence under § 201 of the German Criminal Code (StGB); meeting content can contain personal data under the GDPR (DSGVO). Installation

The steps from “First launch” and the README (Windows 11 x64).

1. Download the four ZIP parts and JoinPortable.cmd from the release page into one folder (about 5.8 GB)
2. Double-click JoinPortable.cmd: it checks sizes and SHA-256 and installs nothing
3. Extract the resulting ZIP completely to an internal SSD
4. Start LectureCompanion.exe (the first start can take a while)
5. Turn on live captions in Teams
Try it without Teams: LectureCompanionDemo.exe

Your data

Captions and slide images are processed locally, with no API key and no cloud fallback. Only Teams and Windows themselves may use the internet.
